PFSA FORENSIC ANALYSIS FEE PAYMENT NOTICE — PUBLIC GOVERNMENT-HOSTED COPY
Reference: https://districtcourtwest.gos.pk/Tender%202021/Payment%20of%20forensic.pdf
Dated: 31 October 2022. PFSA letter No. PFSA/ADMIN-CFS/B/1505.

The notice states that PFSA changed the mode for forensic-analysis fee payment from 32-A challan to Pay Order / Demand Draft titled Director General Punjab Forensic Science Agency Lahore with effect from 01-01-2023, and that 32-A challan payments would not be entertained after 31-12-2022.

IMPORTANT: This notice concerns payment method and does not establish a complete current PFSA service-fee schedule. SceneGuard must not invent fee amounts. Exact current PFSA forensic service charges must be verified from a current official PFSA fee schedule before presentation as authoritative.
