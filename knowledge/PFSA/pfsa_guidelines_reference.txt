PFSA PUBLIC EVIDENCE-GUIDANCE REFERENCE
Public copy: https://www.scribd.com/document/344383168/p-Fsa-Guidelines-English
Official site reference: https://pfsa.punjab.gov.pk/
VERIFICATION: The content is publicly attributable to Punjab Forensic Science Agency material, but the PFSA website was returning an error during September 2026 verification. Treat as a public reference until rechecked against a live PFSA official copy.

Publicly available PFSA guidance material covers Crime Scene Investigation and evidence collection/packaging across forensic disciplines. Recurring themes include scene safety and preservation, controlled access, contemporaneous notes, photography, evidence-specific packaging, contamination avoidance, proper labels, tamper-evident sealing, chain of custody, and discipline-specific submission requirements. Available public material contains sections for crime scene investigation, trace evidence, DNA and serology, latent fingerprints, firearms/toolmarks, questioned documents, pathology, toxicology, digital/audio-video evidence and other laboratory disciplines.

Do not interpret this reference summary as a substitute for the current controlled PFSA SOP/procedure manual.
